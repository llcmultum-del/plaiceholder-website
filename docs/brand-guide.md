# PlAIceholder Brand Guide

Version 1.0 · October 2026

## 1. Name

**PlAIceholder**: a capital P, lowercase "l", capital **AI**, then lowercase "ceholder".

- The "AI" is the point of the name: we fill the places in your week that are waiting for AI.
- Always written as one word with the AI capitalized. Never "Plaiceholder", "PlaiceHolder", "PLAICEHOLDER" or "Placeholder AI".
- Domain: **plaiceholder.io**. In URLs and email addresses it's lowercase.
- Legal entity name: [Legal entity name, e.g. "Multum LLC d/b/a PlAIceholder"]. Use it in contracts and invoices, not in marketing.

## 2. Positioning in one line
Pre-built AI agents that do the busywork inside the tools a growing company already uses, installed, tuned and proven in about two weeks.

### Taglines
- Primary: **"AI agents that do the busywork inside the tools you already use."**
- Hero: **"Your team's slowest weekly task, handed to a custom AI agent in two weeks."**
- Short: **"Installed. Tuned. Proven."**

### Proof points (use these, in this order)
1. Works inside HubSpot, Salesforce, Apollo, Zendesk, Slack, QuickBooks and spreadsheets.
2. Works with the AI you already pay for: Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Grok, Gemini or Copilot.
3. A 20-record pilot with a measured result before the full run.
4. Nothing is written or spent without a yes.
5. You own it.

## 3. Voice
Plain, calm, specific. We sound like a capable operator, not a hype machine.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Built, tested, and running on sample data today." | "Revolutionary AI-powered solutions" |
| "Nothing is written or spent without a yes, one step at a time." | "Fully autonomous, set it and forget it" |
| "Your team sends emails, pays invoices, and makes hiring decisions." | "Replace your team with AI" |
| "Usually a few cents per record. We measure it in your pilot." | "Unlimited AI for one low price!" |
| Numbers, names, dates | Adjectives ("cutting-edge", "game-changing", "seamless") |

Rules:
- Sentences under 25 words. Lead with the task and the outcome, then the technology.
- Say "AI agent". Name the AI platform where trust matters ("runs on Claude, ChatGPT or the AI you already use").
- Never claim results we haven't measured. Real numbers and quotes only, with client permission.
- Second person ("your team"), active voice, US English.

## 4. Color

These match the sample dashboards. If the live website uses different values, the website wins; update this page.

| Role | Name | Hex (light) | Hex (dark mode) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brand accent, links, buttons | Pine | `#2F5D50` | `#7FB8A6` |
| Accent tint (backgrounds, highlights) | Mist | `#E3EEEA` | `#1F3530` |
| Page background | Paper | `#F6F5F1` | `#141413` |
| Surface (cards) | White | `#FFFFFF` | `#1D1D1B` |

| Secondary text | Stone | `#6B6A65` | `#A3A19A` |
| Lines | Line | `#E3E1DA` | `#33322E` |
| Status: hot / high risk | Ember | `#B4432A` | `#F08A6C` |

Rules: one accent per screen. Status colors only carry status, never decoration. Keep text contrast at WCAG AA or better (all pairs above pass on their own background).

## 5. Type
- **Headings and body:** Inter (or the system UI font when Inter isn't available). Headings 600 weight, tight letter spacing (-0.01em).
- **Labels, eyebrows and numbers:** IBM Plex Mono (or a system monospace font), 12px, uppercase, 0.08em letter spacing. Use it for small labels like "SIGNAL PROSPECTOR" above a title.
- **Sizes:** H1 28 to 40px, H2 22 to 28px, body 15 to 17px, captions 12 to 13px.

## 6. Logo
- **Wordmark:** "PlAIceholder" set in Inter Semibold, with **AI** in Pine (`#2F5D50`) and the rest in Ink.
- **Icon / favicon:** "AI" in Pine, white letters on a Pine rounded square for small sizes.
- Clear space equals the height of the "A" on all sides. Minimum width is 96px on screen.
- Don't stretch it, add effects, put it on busy photos, or recolor the "AI" in anything but Pine or white.

## 7. Imagery
- Show the product: dashboards, before-and-after tables, the 5-step diagram (Pull, Think, Check, Write back, Show and ask).
- Simple line diagrams in Ink and Pine. No stock robots, glowing brains, circuit boards or people pointing at screens.
- Sample data is always labeled: "Sample data. Companies shown are fictional."

## 8. Naming agents
- Pattern: plain function plus a noun: "Signal Prospector", "Renewal Risk Radar", "Invoice Checker".
- Say what it does in 2 or 3 words. No made-up words, no "-ify", no "GPT" in names (it's a competitor's mark).
- In copy: "Signal Prospector finds companies showing buying signals…", always starting with the job.

## 9. Documents and deliverables
- Every client deliverable starts with a README stating the client name, agent, version and "Licensed to [Client] for internal use only. Built by PlAIceholder."
- Proposals, SOWs and reports use the same colors and fonts. The title page has the wordmark, document title, client and date.
- File names: `client-agent-vN` (for example `acme-signal-prospector-v2.zip`).
