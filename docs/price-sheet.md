| | Starter | Standard | Advanced |

In exchange: a written testimonial, permission to publish a measured result, and a
6-month care plan commitment (3 included + 3 paid).

## Every care plan includes
- Monitoring of every run and a monthly results email (records processed, findings, cost per record)
- Fixes when a connected tool or AI model changes
- Moves to newer AI models when the current one is retired
- Tuning hours (above) for prompts, scoring, signals and settings
- Response within 2 business days
- **Fix guarantee:** if a change in a connected tool breaks your agent, we fix it within 5 business days, or that month's care fee is waived.

## Discounts and terms
- **Second and later agents at the same company:** 25% off setup and 25% off that agent's care plan.
- **Annual care prepay:** 12 months for the price of 10.
- **Payment:** launch package 50% at kickoff, 50% at handover. Care billed monthly in advance from month 4.
- **Out-of-scope work:** $125/hour, or a fixed quote for larger changes.
- **New kind of agent (custom):** quoted per project; typically Standard or Advanced pricing plus $1,000 to $3,000 for building the new template.
- **Data cleanup:** quoted separately (or start with the CRM Hygiene agent).

## Two ways to use every agent (no price difference)
- **Chat mode:** a ready-to-paste agent for the client's AI tool (Claude Project, ChatGPT GPT, Perplexity Space, Grok, Gemini Gem, Copilot agent).
- **Automated mode:** runs on a schedule and writes results into the client's CRM, help desk or Slack.
