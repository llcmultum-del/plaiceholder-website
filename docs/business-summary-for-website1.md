## 2. The business in one paragraph

[Business Name] builds and installs pre-built AI agents for companies with 10 to 200
employees. Each agent takes over one slow, repetitive judgment task, such as researching
prospects, spotting customers about to churn, or checking invoices before they're paid. It
works inside tools the company already pays for: HubSpot, Salesforce, Apollo, Zendesk,
Slack, QuickBooks or spreadsheet exports. Each agent is tuned to the business through a
short interview, proven on a 20-record pilot with a measured result, and handed over with
training. It runs in the client's own accounts with their own keys, and they own it. A
small monthly care plan keeps it working as their tools change.

## 3. Positioning

- **Tagline options:**
  - "AI agents that do the busywork inside the tools you already use."
  - "Your team's slowest weekly task, handed to an AI agent in two weeks."
  - "Pre-built AI agents for growing companies. Installed, tuned, proven."
- **Category:** done-for-you AI agents and automation for small and mid-size businesses.
- **Who it's for:** companies with enough budget to outsource a project, but not enough to hire a full-time AI or prompt engineer.
- **Buyers:** owners, sales leaders, heads of operations, customer success leads, controllers and AP managers.
- **The problem:** skilled people spend hours a week on research, data entry and checking work. AI could do it, but nobody on the team has time to build it, test it and keep it working.
- **The promise:** one agent, one painful task, live in about two weeks, with a measured before-and-after.
- **Why us, not an agency:** agents are pre-built, so we sell setup and tuning, not months of custom development. Agencies quoted one comparable agent at $9,500. Ours start at $1,500.
- **Why us, not a hire:** a US automation engineer costs roughly $90,000 to $140,000 a year in salary alone ([Evolv](https://evolvaiagents.com/blog/how-much-does-an-ai-automation-agency-cost-in-2026-real-pricing-breakdown/)). Our care plans start at $250 a month.
- **Why us, not do-it-yourself:** off-the-shelf AI features are generic. Ours are tuned to how your team sells, serves and pays, and every finding is checked before anyone sees it.

## 4. How every agent works (5 steps, show as a diagram)

1. **Pull:** gets data from the tools you already pay for.
2. **Think:** AI does the research, reading or judgment a person used to do. That can be Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Grok, Gemini or Copilot, whichever your team already uses.
3. **Check:** rules catch AI mistakes before anyone sees them. No source link means no claim. No real ticket means no reason. Hard rules always come first.
4. **Write back:** results go where your team already works, such as CRM fields, notes, Slack or a report.
5. **Show and ask:** a simple dashboard for the team, and a person says yes before anything is spent or changed.

## 5. Safety and trust (a "Built to be trusted" section)

- **Dry run by default:** nothing is written or spent without a yes, one step at a time.
- **Pilot first:** every agent proves itself on 20 records before a full run.
- **No made-up facts:** every AI finding links to its source or evidence. Unsupported findings are thrown out automatically.
- **Spending caps:** limits on AI usage and data credits, set in plain settings.
- **Never deletes:** agents only add or update their own fields, and back up every record before changing it.
- **You own it:** it runs in your accounts with your keys, licensed to you for internal use. At handover we remove our setup access and delete our copies of your data. If you keep a care plan, you give us a separate, limited login you can switch off at any time.
- **Your AI, your choice:** the same agent works in Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Grok, Gemini or Copilot, as a chat assistant or running automatically.
- **A full paper trail:** every run keeps a log, the results and a backup.
- **People decide:** agents draft, flag and score. Your team sends emails, pays invoices and makes hiring decisions.

### Why a monthly care plan (a short section on the pricing page)
1. **Your tools change under the agent.** HubSpot, Salesforce and Apollo update their APIs, and AI models get retired every few months.
2. **Quiet failure is the real risk.** A crash is obvious. A data source drying up or scores drifting isn't. We watch the results every month.
3. **Your business changes.** Tuning hours keep the agent matched to how you sell and serve today.
4. **Proof every month.** A results report shows what it processed, what it found and what it cost per record.
5. **Guaranteed.** If a tool change breaks it, it's fixed in 5 business days or that month is free.

## 10. FAQ

- **What exactly is an "AI agent"?** A program that does a multi-step task a person used to do, like research, reading, judging and writing up, inside your tools, with rules that check its work.
- **Do we need to be technical?** No. You need an admin who can create access keys and an hour from the people who do the task.
- **Which AI do you use?** Whichever your team already pays for: Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Grok, Gemini or Copilot. It runs through your own account, so you see and control the usage bill.
- **Can we use it in ChatGPT (or Claude, Gemini, Copilot)?** Yes. Every agent comes as a ready-to-paste assistant for your AI tool, and optionally as an automated version that runs on a schedule and writes into your CRM.
- **What does it cost to run?** You pay AI usage and data credits directly at cost, usually a few cents per record. We measure the real number in your pilot.
- **Who owns it?** You do. It runs in your accounts with your keys, licensed for your internal use (our templates can't be resold). We may reuse our general templates for other clients, never your data or settings.
- **What happens to our data?** It stays in your systems. At handover we remove our setup access and delete any copies. With a care plan, you give us a separate, limited login that you can switch off at any time. When the plan ends, we're out completely.
- **What if HubSpot or another tool changes?** That's what the care plan covers. Without one, fixes are billed hourly.
- **Can the AI send emails or pay invoices on its own?** No. Agents draft, score and flag, and people approve.
- **What if the pilot doesn't work?** You see the result after 20 records, before the full run. If the result isn't useful, we tune it or stop.
- **Why is there a monthly plan if we own the agent?** You do own it. The plan isn't for access; it's for keeping it working while your tools and AI models change, plus tuning, a monthly report and a fix guarantee. Your first 3 months are included.
- **Can we cancel the care plan?** Yes, after month 3, with 30 days' notice. The agent keeps running; you just lose monitoring, fixes and tuning.
- **Our CRM is messy. Is that a problem?** It can be. We quote cleanup separately, or start with the CRM Hygiene agent.

## 13. ROI calculator (formula)
Inputs: people doing the task (P), hours per week each (H), hourly cost (C, default $45), share of time saved (S, default 50%), tier (launch package and care from section 7).
- Hours saved per month = P × H × S × 4.33
- Dollars saved per month = hours saved × C
- Payback in weeks = launch package ÷ (dollars saved per month ÷ 4.33)   (care is included for the first 3 months)
- Year-one return = 12 × dollars saved per month − launch package − 9 × care

Label it "Estimate. Your pilot measures the real number."
