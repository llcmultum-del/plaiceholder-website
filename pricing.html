Pricing rules

A few things worth knowing

Launch package

Every engagement starts with the launch package: setup plus the first 3 months of care in one fixed price. After month 3 it rolls to a month-to-month plan, cancelable with 30 days' notice.

Founding-client pricing

Available for the first 5 clients in exchange for a written testimonial, a published result, and a 6-month care plan commitment.

Second agent discount

25% off setup and the first 3 months of care for a second agent at the same company. The connections are already in place.

Annual care plan

Pay for 10 months and get 12. Equivalent to 2 months free.

Usage at cost

You pay AI (Claude) usage and data credits (e.g. Apollo) directly to those providers. Typical AI usage is a few cents per record. We estimate them up front and measure the real number in the pilot.

Extra work

$125/hour for work outside the care plan scope. If you cancel the care plan and later need the agent fixed or updated, a reactivation fee applies ($500 Starter · $750 Standard · $1,000 Advanced).

Payment

50% at kickoff, 50% at handover. Care plan billed monthly from month 4 onward.

Access and privacy

At handover we remove our setup access and delete any copies of your data. If you keep a care plan, you give us a separate, limited login you can switch off at any time. When the plan ends, we're out completely.

FAQ

Common questions

What exactly is an "AI agent"?

A program that does a multi-step task a person used to do — research, reading, judging, and writing up — inside your tools, with rules that check its work before anyone sees it.

Do we need to be technical?

No. You need an admin who can create access keys and one hour from the people who currently do the task.

Which AI do you use?

Claude, by Anthropic, running through your own account. You see and control the usage bill directly.

What does it cost to run?

You pay AI usage and data credits directly to those providers at cost — typically a few cents per record. We estimate the number up front and measure the real cost per record in your pilot.

Who owns it?

You do. The agent runs in your accounts with your keys. It's licensed for your internal use — our templates can't be resold. We keep the right to reuse our general methodology for other clients, but never your data or settings.

What happens to our data?

It stays in your systems. At handover we remove our setup access and delete any copies of your data. If you keep a care plan, you give us a separate, limited login you can switch off at any time. When the plan ends, we're out completely.

Why do I need a care plan?

AI tools and the models behind them change constantly — often quietly. A prompt that worked in January may drift by April. The care plan catches this with monthly monitoring, keeps fixes within 5 business days, and gives you a monthly results email so you can see if output quality changes before it affects your team.

What if HubSpot or another tool changes?

That's what the care plan covers: fixes within 5 business days, guaranteed, or that month is free. Without a plan, fixes are billed at $125/hour.

What if I cancel the care plan and later need the agent fixed?

We can reactivate it. A reactivation fee applies ($500 for Starter, $750 for Standard, $1,000 for Advanced) to cover the time needed to bring it back up to date.

Can the AI send emails or pay invoices on its own?

No. Agents draft, score, and flag. People approve.

What if the pilot doesn't work?

You see the result after 20 records, before the full run. If the result isn't useful, we tune it or stop.

Our CRM is messy. Is that a problem?

It can be. We quote cleanup separately, or you can start with the CRM Hygiene agent.
