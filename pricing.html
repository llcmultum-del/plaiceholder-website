A few things worth knowing

Founding-client pricing

Available for the first 5 clients in exchange for a written testimonial, a published result, and a 6-month care plan.

Second agent discount

25% off setup for a second agent at the same company. The connections are already in place.

Annual care plan

Pay for 10 months and get 12. Equivalent to 2 months free.

Usage at cost

You pay AI model usage and data credits (e.g. Apollo) directly to those providers. Typical AI usage is a few cents per record. We estimate them up front and measure the real number in the pilot.

Extra work

$125/hour for work outside the care plan, or a new build fee for a brand-new kind of agent.

FAQ

Common questions

What exactly is an "AI agent"?

A program that does a multi-step task a person used to do, research, reading, judging, and writing up, inside your tools, with rules that check its work before anyone sees it.

Do we need to be technical?

No. You need an admin who can create access keys and one hour from the people who currently do the task.

Which AI do you use?

We build with the leading models, Claude, GPT-4o, Gemini, and others, and pick whichever fits your stack and budget best. The model runs through your own account so you see and control the usage bill directly.

What does it cost to run?

You pay AI usage and data credits directly to those providers at cost, typically a few cents per record. We estimate the number up front and measure the real cost per record in your pilot.

Who owns it?

You do. The agent runs in your accounts with your keys. We keep the right to reuse our general templates for other clients, but never your data or settings.

What happens to our data?

It stays in your systems. We delete any copies and our access at handover.

What if HubSpot or another tool changes?

That's what the care plan covers. Without one, fixes are billed at $125/hour.

Can the AI send emails or pay invoices on its own?

No. Agents draft, score, and flag. People approve.

What if the pilot doesn't work?

You see the result after 20 records, before the full run. If the result isn't useful, we tune it or stop.

Can we cancel the care plan?

Yes, with 30 days' notice.

Our CRM is messy. Is that a problem?

It can be. We quote cleanup separately, or you can start with the CRM Hygiene agent.
