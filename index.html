How every agent works

Five steps, every time

Every agent follows the same pattern, no matter what job it does.

1

Pull

Gets data from the tools you already pay for — CRM, help desk, accounting, spreadsheets.

2

Think

Claude (by Anthropic) does the research, reading, or judgment a person used to do.

3

Check

Rules catch AI mistakes before anyone sees them. No source link means no claim. Hard rules always come first.

4

Write back

Results go where your team already works — CRM fields, notes, Slack, or a report.

5

Show and ask

A dashboard for the team, and a person says yes before anything is spent or changed.
