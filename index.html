What we automate

If a person does it every week, an agent can do it for them.

Any repeatable task that involves reading, judging, or writing is a candidate. Here are the most common types.

Reading and research

Prospect research, buying-signal monitoring, meeting prep briefs, competitor tracking. The agent reads, summarizes, and surfaces only what matters.

Scoring and flagging

Lead fit scoring, renewal risk, invoice exceptions, ticket priority. The agent runs the same judgment call every time and flags anything that needs a human look.

Writing and reporting

Draft follow-up emails, weekly KPI summaries, RFP answers, applicant shortlists. The agent writes the first version; your team reviews and sends.
