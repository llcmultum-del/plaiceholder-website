How It Works

Two weeks from call to running agent. Here's what happens at each step — and why we do it that way.

Why not just hire someone?

Three ways to get AI doing real work. One that actually delivers.

Most companies try one of three paths. Two of them stall.

Hire an AI person

$120K+ salary. 3–6 months to ramp. Spends half their time learning new tools. If they leave, the agent breaks. You're funding their education.

Buy an off-the-shelf tool

Works for the demo use case, not yours. Doesn't connect to your specific CRM setup. Requires someone to run it. Subscription stacks up fast.

Work with {PlAIceholder}

Fixed price. Two weeks. Built around exactly how your team works. Runs in your own tools. We stay on it so it keeps working when things change.

The process

Every step is fixed price, no surprises.

30 min — Free

Live demo call

We show you a working agent on sample data. You tell us the task that costs your team the most time. No pitch deck, no commitment. If we can't help, we'll say so.

1–2 days

Fixed-price proposal

We send a scope document: exactly what the agent will do, what it won't do, what it connects to, the price, and the timeline. 50% to start, 50% at handover.

1 hour

Discovery interview

We interview the person who currently does the task. Their answers become the agent's settings: which signals matter, what a good result looks like, what edge cases to handle, what to skip. This hour is what makes it yours, not generic.

3–5 days

Connect and pilot

You create API keys in your existing tools; we do the rest. We run the agent on 20 real records. Your team reviews the top results and tells us what to tune before the full run.

1 day

Measure and hand over

We time the old way against the new way on the same 20 records, write up the real numbers, and run a 30-minute training. You pay the remaining 50%. The agent is yours.

Ongoing

Care plan

We monitor the agent, fix it when a connected tool changes its API, apply tuning hours each month, and send a monthly results email. You're not maintaining it alone.

What we need from you

Three things — that's it.

An admin who can create access keys

We connect through read-only or scoped API keys in your existing tools. You control the keys and can revoke them at any time. We never ask for more access than the specific task requires.

One hour from the person who does the task

This is the most important hour. We learn what "good" looks like, what signals matter, what exceptions exist. The discovery interview is what separates an agent that fits your business from one that fits a demo.

Clean-enough data

Your data doesn't need to be perfect. If it needs cleanup first, we quote that separately — or start with the CRM Hygiene agent to get it in shape before the main build.

Safety rules

Not optional. Not configurable. Built in.

Every agent ships with the same rules. These protect your data, your budget, and your team's trust in the output.

Pilot first, always

Every agent proves itself on 20 records before touching your full list. If the pilot doesn't meet your bar, we fix it before the full run.

No made-up facts

Every AI finding requires a source link it actually retrieved. Findings without evidence are discarded before your team sees them — automatically, not by review.

Hard spending caps

AI usage costs and data-credit budgets are set in plain settings and enforced before every run. The agent can't overspend.

Never deletes, always backs up

Agents only write to their own designated fields. Every record is backed up before any change is made.

People make the final call

Agents draft, score, and flag. Your team sends the email, pays the invoice, and makes the hire. No fully automated irreversible actions.

Full audit trail

Every run keeps a log: inputs, AI reasoning, rule outcomes, final results. You can replay or audit any run at any time.

Your data stays yours

The agent runs in your accounts with your keys. We delete our working copies at handover. You own the agent and everything it produces.

Dry run before every change

Anything that modifies your data runs in read-only mode first. Your team sees the proposed changes before a single record is updated.

See it working before you decide.

The demo call is 30 minutes, free, and runs on sample data. No pitch deck. If we can't help, we'll tell you that too.
