What it is & who it's for

What is a custom AI agent for business?

A custom AI agent is software that handles one specific, repeatable task your team does regularly, without a human doing it manually. It reads from your systems, makes decisions based on rules you approve, writes or flags the output, and hands the result back to your team. It's not a general assistant and it's not a chatbot. It does one job, inside the tools you already use, on a schedule you control.

How is an AI agent different from a chatbot or Zapier automation?

A chatbot responds to questions in a conversation window. A Zapier automation moves data from point A to point B when a trigger fires. An AI agent reasons. It reads unstructured text, weighs multiple signals, makes a judgment, and produces a structured output. For example: a Zap can copy a lead into your CRM when a form is submitted. An AI agent can read that lead's LinkedIn profile, their company's recent news, and their job title, then score the opportunity and write a personalized outreach draft. The judgment is what makes it an agent.

Is PlAIceholder a good fit for a small or mid-sized business?

Yes. We work best with companies that have a clear repeatable task taking meaningful staff hours every week, a team willing to review outputs in the first few weeks, and an appetite to actually use the results. If you can describe the task in a paragraph and name the person who currently does it, we can probably automate a large part of it. We don't require a large IT team or existing AI infrastructure.

Do I need an in-house AI engineer to use an AI agent?

No. We handle the build, the integration, and the initial tuning. Once deployed, the agent runs automatically and your team just reviews the outputs in the same tools they already use. If something needs adjustment, you contact us and we handle it. The care plan exists specifically so you don't need to maintain it yourself.

What's the difference between hiring an AI agency and building in-house?

Building in-house means hiring an ML engineer or AI developer, spending months on trials, and owning all the maintenance. It makes sense if AI is core to your product. For a business task like lead scoring or invoice review, you're paying for a capability, not building a platform. We deliver a working agent in roughly two weeks at a fixed price, with a care plan that keeps it working. No recruiting, no six-month build cycle, no internal expertise required.

Sales

Can AI find companies that are ready to buy?

It can surface companies showing signals that often precede a buying decision: job postings that suggest a problem you solve, news mentions of relevant events, funding announcements, leadership changes, or other observable indicators. It won't predict the future, but it narrows a large list to the accounts most worth calling this week. That's what our Signal Prospector does.

How does AI lead research work?

The agent takes a list of companies or contacts, pulls publicly available information from web sources, company sites, LinkedIn, and news feeds, then synthesizes a brief on each one: who they are, why they might be a fit, what to reference in an outreach. The output lands in your CRM or a spreadsheet, structured and ready to use. Research that takes a rep 30 minutes per account can be done in under a minute per account at scale.

Can an AI agent follow up on stalled deals in HubSpot or Salesforce?

Yes. The agent scans deals that have gone quiet based on the last activity date, drafts a personalized follow-up for each one based on the deal context and contact history, and queues the drafts for your rep to review and send. It doesn't send automatically unless you specifically want that. The rep stays in the loop; they just stop starting from a blank page.

What is a buying signal, and how does the Signal Prospector find them?

A buying signal is any observable event that suggests a company may be in a position to buy. Common examples: a job posting for a role that implies they need what you sell, a press release about a problem you solve, a funding round that gives them budget, or a leadership hire who has bought similar solutions before. The Signal Prospector monitors sources you define, extracts these events, scores them against your ideal customer profile, and delivers a ranked list each week.

Customer Success

Can AI predict which customers won't renew?

It can flag accounts showing risk indicators before the renewal conversation. These signals include declining usage, unresolved support tickets, reduced stakeholder engagement, or CRM notes that mention competitor evaluation. It won't be right every time, but it surfaces the accounts most worth a proactive conversation while there's still time to act. That's what the Renewal Risk Radar does.

How does AI triage support tickets in Zendesk?

The agent reads incoming tickets, classifies them by issue type and urgency, routes them to the right queue or team, and drafts an initial response for the agent to review. It can also link tickets to known issues or documentation. The goal is to cut first-response time and reduce the number of tickets your team has to read in full before knowing what to do with them.

What does the Renewal Risk Radar actually flag?

Accounts where one or more risk signals are present: low product usage relative to their contract, open support issues past a certain age, no recorded activity with the account team in the last 60 days, NPS below a threshold, or notes mentioning dissatisfaction or competitor evaluation. Each flag includes the evidence so your CSM can make a judgment call, not just a warning light with no context.

Back Office

Can AI check invoices against purchase orders automatically?

Yes. The Invoice Checker reads incoming invoices, matches them against the corresponding purchase orders, and flags discrepancies: amounts that don't match, vendors not on your approved list, line items that don't appear on the PO, or invoices submitted more than once. Each flagged invoice includes the specific mismatch so your AP team can resolve it rather than hunt for it. Clean invoices pass through without manual review.

Does the Invoice Checker work with QuickBooks?

We've built integrations with QuickBooks, as well as email-based invoice workflows where PDFs or attachments arrive in a shared inbox. The specific integration depends on how you currently receive and process invoices. We cover this on the discovery call and confirm the integration before we build anything.

Can AI screen job applicants fairly?

The Resume Screener scores applicants against criteria you define: required skills, experience level, specific qualifications. It doesn't make hiring decisions and it doesn't have opinions about names, photos, or background. Every score includes the reasoning so a human reviewer can agree or disagree. We recommend treating it as a first-pass tool that reduces the volume your team reads in full, not as a replacement for human judgment in hiring.

Can an AI agent write weekly KPI reports?

Yes. The Weekly KPI Writer pulls your numbers from connected sources, compares them to the prior period and targets, writes the narrative summary, and delivers a formatted report to your inbox or Slack on a schedule. The report is ready to send or present with minimal editing. It's one of our most popular agents because it saves 1 to 3 hours every single week without any change to how you collect your data.

Safety & Trust

Is it safe to let AI touch my company's data?

We take this seriously. The agent only accesses the specific data needed for the task. We use encrypted connections, don't store your data beyond what's needed to run the agent, and don't use your data to train any AI model. Every integration is scoped to the minimum permissions required. We'll walk you through exactly what the agent reads and writes before anything goes live.

Can the AI agent delete or overwrite my records?

By default, no. Every agent we build is configured to write new records or add fields, not to delete or overwrite existing data. If your use case genuinely requires overwriting a field, we discuss it explicitly and document it in the proposal. Auto-delete is never on by default and requires a deliberate decision on your part.

How do you stop AI from hallucinating or making things up?

We design agents to work from sources, not from memory. Every output cites the specific text or record it was based on. The agent is not asked to generate facts; it's asked to extract, classify, or summarize from real inputs. For tasks where accuracy is critical, we also include a confidence score so reviewers know when to double-check. No agent we build makes an unsourced claim as if it were fact.

What is dry-run mode?

Dry-run mode lets the agent process real data and produce its full output without writing anything back to your systems. You see exactly what it would have done without any of it actually happening. We use dry-run on the pilot: the agent runs on 20 real records, you review the results, and you decide whether the quality meets your bar before we flip it to live. It's how we prove the agent works before you rely on it.

Who owns the AI agent after it's built?

You do. We hand over the agent, the configuration, and documentation of how it works. If you cancel the care plan and want to run it independently, you can. We don't lock you into our infrastructure. The care plan is a service, not a lease.

Do you keep a copy of my company's data?

No. We don't retain your records after the agent has processed them. Logs used for debugging are kept for a limited period and then deleted. We don't use your data to train models, build datasets, or improve our general product. What you share with us stays in the agent and your systems.

How do I control how much the AI spends?

Every agent runs with a spending cap. You set a maximum number of records per run, a maximum number of API calls, or both. The agent stops and alerts you if it's about to exceed the limit. The API cost for a typical agent run is small and predictable. We'll give you an estimate of monthly API cost as part of the proposal, and the care plan includes monitoring to catch any unexpected spikes.

It depends on the task and how often your team does it. For most projects, between one and four months. The payback period depends on how many hours the agent saves per week and what those hours cost. The ROI calculator on our pricing page will give you a specific estimate for your situation. You know what you're paying before we start. It also forces us to scope clearly upfront, which produces better agents than open-ended development does.

Process & Timeline

How long does it take to build an AI agent?

About two weeks from signed proposal to pilot results. Week one is the discovery interview, integration setup, and build. Week two is the pilot run on 20 real records, your review, and any tuning needed before full deployment. The two-week timeline assumes reasonable access to your systems and one point of contact on your side. Unusually complex integrations can extend this to three to four weeks.

What happens on the free 30-minute call?

We'll ask you to describe the task you want to automate: what currently happens, who does it, what systems are involved, and what a good output looks like. We'll show you a live demo of the most relevant agent in our catalog. Then we'll tell you honestly whether we think an agent is a good fit, what it would cost, and what the timeline would look like. No prepared pitch. No pressure. If it's not the right fit, we'll tell you that too.

What is a pilot run, and why 20 records?

The pilot is the agent running on 20 real records from your data, in dry-run mode so nothing is written back yet. You review the outputs alongside the actual records and tell us whether the quality is acceptable. Twenty is enough to catch edge cases and calibration issues without requiring a large sample. If the pilot meets your bar, we flip to live. If it doesn't, we adjust and run again. You don't pay the second half of the setup until the pilot passes.

What happens if the agent stops working or needs changes?

Contact us. That's what the care plan covers. Tool APIs change, models improve, and your business needs evolve. We monitor for failures and usually catch problems before you notice them. For changes to scope or new functionality, we quote those separately. Bug fixes, maintenance, and keeping the existing agent running are covered in the monthly fee.

Can I start with one agent and add more later?

Yes, and that's how most clients start. Pick the task that takes the most time or creates the most risk, automate that first, and then assess. The integrations we set up for the first agent often make subsequent agents faster to build. There's no requirement to commit to multiple agents upfront.
