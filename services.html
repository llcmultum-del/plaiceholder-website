AI Consultation

What a consultation looks like

We spend time understanding your business before we recommend anything.

Week 1

Workflow interviews

We interview the people doing the work — sales, ops, CS, finance. We learn what they actually do each week, where time goes, and what frustrates them most.

Week 2

Opportunity mapping

We map every repeatable task against three criteria: time cost, AI suitability, and implementation risk. This becomes your automation opportunity map.

Delivery

Prioritized roadmap

A written report with your top 3 to 5 automation opportunities ranked by expected ROI, an honest assessment of complexity, and a recommended starting point.

Optional

Ongoing advisory

Monthly retainer for teams that want a dedicated AI advisor to evaluate new tools, review prompt quality, and help prioritize the roadmap as the business changes.
